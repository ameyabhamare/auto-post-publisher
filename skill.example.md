# Writing style: <your name>

Copy this file to `skill.md` and replace everything below with how you actually write.
The more specific you are, the more the drafts will sound like you.

## Voice
- e.g. First person, conversational, a bit dry. Writes like explaining something to a friend over coffee.
- e.g. Confident but not preachy. Admits when unsure.

## Structure
- e.g. Opens with a concrete moment or observation, never with a definition or "In today's world…".
- e.g. Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). Uses `##` subheadings only for posts over ~800 words.
- e.g. Ends with a takeaway or an open question, not a summary of what was just said.

## Length
- e.g. 500–900 words unless the content clearly needs more.

## Word choice
- e.g. Plain words over jargon. Indian English spelling (colour, organise).
- Avoid: "delve", "game-changer", "in conclusion", "unlock", excessive exclamation marks, emoji.

## Images
- e.g. One image near the top. Short italic captions under photos.

## Things I never do
- e.g. Never use listicle titles ("7 ways to…").
- e.g. Never add hashtags.
